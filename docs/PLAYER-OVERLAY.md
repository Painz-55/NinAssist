# Player position overlay (experimental, read-only)

The normal Substitution Technique now creates a decoy/invisibility and is **not** a SubPunish trigger. Only exact `Speed Mirage Technique` (Tai) and `Water Substitution Technique` buffs are eligible for the live early-removal heuristic. Disappearance is still not proof of a hit, teleport or server acceptance. The older signed profiles and installed build are unchanged by this source update.

The overlay uses the existing client-side `MapPlayers` records. It starts OFF and requires MapPlayers access, an exact-pair signed profile, a live frame, verified map-grid dimensions, and a game render view mapped to the **exact** 2026-11-15 or 2026-11-16 DLL hash. In **PLAYERS NO MAPA**, click the overlay button once to enable it and again to disable it; no F10 calibration. It considers records throughout the client map (not a 12/30-tile radius), then draws only points falling inside the observed `NinEngine.Graphics.View` center/size and game window, including camera shifts near map edges and client scaling. Unknown or inconsistent view data fails closed instead of falling back to a guessed player-centered camera. Reads are requested at 80 ms intervals. F12 turns it OFF. It does not select targets, send input, trigger attacks or alter the game process.

This is a **last-known client-record marker**, not an invisible-person detector: positions can be missing, frozen or represent a decoy. The 2026-11-16 passive observation of Raizen Kaen and KageSlayer plus the user's identification of the translucent player and red decoy supports the coordinate mapping in one scene, not universally. A later 45-second passive capture observed the Kage record present in 307/307 frames but at a constant coordinate for about 40 seconds after the reported normal Sub at ~5 s. A separate numeric-field survey during another Sub observed coordinate movement, but the user clarified this could be the final server sync after the effect, **not intermediate invisible movement**. Do not interpolate that route. The overlay always labels its dot “última pos.” and displays time without observed displacement. If the record disappears from otherwise fresh map lists, the last location is gray with its observation age and expires after 7 seconds. A marker is hidden if the list is older than 300 ms, the frame/identity/map/grid/view is invalid, or the game loses foreground. The actual render-view center/size fixed a visible-player alignment problem per user feedback; invisible tracking is not validated. Never use a marker as proof of a hit or to automate target choice.

The first local trial was rejected: F10 calibration was unwanted, and Water SubPunish did not dispatch. Its log observed Water at 5 s, but also showed missing selected-player gate and lost game focus; it did not prove an early Water removal. The revised engine may watch Tai/Water before a target is selected; its adapter still requires a stable selected living player and game foreground before **every** offensive key. A later 2026-11-16 integrated trial logged a Tai combo with a selected player after heuristic early disappearance. The user confirmed the revised local app worked, but the trace cannot prove a hit, the removal cause, or universal behavior.
